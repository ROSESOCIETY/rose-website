Our Programs

ROSE works to support people when they need help and empower them with the knowledge, skills and opportunities they need to move towards a better and more independent life.

Our Areas of Support
Essential Support Education & Child Development Health & Well-being Women Empowerment Skills & Opportunities Livelihoods & Employment Social Empowerment

Essential Support

We provide support to individuals and families who are going through difficult circumstances and need help with their basic needs. Our support may include food, clothing, household essentials, educational necessities and other immediate requirements.

We aim to provide timely help while treating every person with care, dignity and respect.

Education & Child Development

We believe every child should have the opportunity to learn, grow and build a better future. We support children who may face difficulties in continuing their education by helping with school-related needs, books, learning materials, educational expenses and other necessary support.

We also encourage activities that contribute to a child's confidence, learning and overall development.

Health & Well-being

Good health is an important part of a secure and dignified life. We support individuals and families who need help with basic healthcare, medical assistance, medicines, health check-ups and health camps.

We also promote awareness about healthy practices and encourage people to seek timely medical care when needed.

Women Empowerment

We believe women should have the confidence, knowledge and opportunities to make decisions about their own lives. ROSE supports women through awareness, guidance, learning opportunities and initiatives that encourage independence and participation.

Our aim is to help women strengthen their confidence and create better opportunities for themselves and their families.

Skills & Opportunities

Learning a practical skill can open the door to new opportunities. We provide opportunities for people to learn useful skills such as tailoring, embroidery, handicrafts, computer skills, digital skills, communication and other vocational skills.

We aim to make learning accessible and useful, so that people can use their skills in everyday life, further training or future work opportunities.

Livelihoods & Employment

A stable source of income can make a significant difference to an individual and their family. We support people in exploring employment, self-employment and small income-generating opportunities based on their abilities and circumstances.

Our focus is on helping people move towards greater financial stability and become more self-reliant.

Social Empowerment

Social empowerment begins with awareness and the ability to make informed choices. We help people understand their rights, available government services, welfare schemes, support systems and opportunities that may be available to them.

We encourage individuals and families to seek the support they are entitled to, make informed decisions and become more confident in handling their everyday lives.
